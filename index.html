What we are building

Inatico is a collaboration to bring forward the future of software and interface design.

We work across social platforms, trading models, and a deliberate use of AI, creating value on more than one front.

It began when we stepped away from our digital marketing platform. We wanted something larger, and something we believe in. That is where Inatico was born.

We take on problems from several directions, with one aim: to build what comes next. Our current focus is Studio 16, a private platform for investing together.
